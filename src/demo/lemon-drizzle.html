Lemon drizzle cake

A sharp, sticky loaf cake. Makes 10 slices. 15 minutes to prepare, 45 minutes in the oven.

Ingredients

  • 225g unsalted butter, softened
  • 225g caster sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 225g self-raising flour
  • Zest of 2 lemons
  • Juice of 2 lemons
  • 85g granulated sugar

Method

  1. Heat the oven to 180C (160C fan). Line a 2lb loaf tin with baking paper.
  2. Beat the butter and caster sugar together until pale and fluffy, about 3 minutes.
  3. Add the eggs one at a time, beating well after each.
  4. Fold in the flour and the lemon zest until no streaks of flour remain.
  5. Spoon the batter into the tin and level the top.
  6. Bake for 30 minutes, then check: a skewer should come out clean. Give it another 10 to 15 minutes if not.
  7. Mix the lemon juice with the granulated sugar. While the cake is still warm, prick it all over with a fork and pour the drizzle over. Leave to cool in the tin for 20 minutes before turning out.
